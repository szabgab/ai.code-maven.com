Not All Invariants Are Equal: Curating Training Data to Accelerate Program Verification with Small Language Models

Proving that a program is correct usually depends on finding the right loop invariants: facts that stay true every time a loop runs. Language models can now suggest them. But which training examples actually teach a model to do this well? In this talk I'll show how carefully curating the training data, rather than just adding more of it, lets small language models find useful invariants faster and makes automated program verification more practical. Based on our ICML 2026 paper, a collaboration between the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and VMware Research by Broadcom, joint work with Yizhak Yisrael Elboher, Haoze Wu, Nina Narodytska and Guy Katz.

[Paper](https://idopinto.github.io/wonda-project-page)
